Outdoor Games Guide: Rules & Player Counts

1. Football (Soccer)

Number of Players: 11 players per team (22 total on the field)

Basic Rules:

  • Players must not use their hands or arms to touch the ball (except for the goalkeeper within their penalty area).
  • A standard match lasts for 90 minutes, split into two 45-minute halves.
  • The team that scores the highest number of goals by the end of the match wins.

2. Cricket

Number of Players: 11 players per team (22 total on the field)

Basic Rules:

  • The batting team aims to score runs while the bowling/fielding team tries to restrict runs and dismiss batters.
  • An inning ends when 10 batsmen are out or the designated number of overs is completed.
  • The team with the highest total score wins the match.

3. Basketball

Number of Players: 5 players per team (10 total on the court)

Basic Rules:

  • Players must move the ball down the court by bouncing it (dribbling) or passing it to teammates.
  • Points are scored by shooting the ball through the opponent's hoop (basket).
  • The team with the most points at the end of four quarters wins.

4. Volleyball

Number of Players: 6 players per team (12 total on the court)

Basic Rules:

  • Teams hit the ball over a net, aiming to make it land on the opponent's side of the court.
  • A team is allowed a maximum of 3 touches to return the ball over the net.
  • Matches are typically played as the best of 5 sets (first to reach 25 points in a set).
